BUBIX Middle East Business Plan v3.2 / English edition 2026-10-28

DIRECTION
Use the UAE as the primary development hub. Connect national-level infrastructure, enterprise applications and ongoing operations. Regional responsibilities include access, proposals, contracts, delivery coordination and cash collection.

YEAR-ONE REVENUE
The supplier scenario assumes two enterprise platforms, one national phase-one project and preliminary services: USD 4.8m contract value, USD 3.6375m recognized revenue and USD 4.25m collections. These are assumptions, not signed orders or approved KPIs. Without national phase one, supplier revenue falls to USD 1.7625m. Scope, engineering cost, pricing, timing and acceptance support the model; unverified historical revenue is not a target anchor.

INVESTMENT AND ATTRIBUTION
USD 5-10m is a proposed initial capital range; funding entity, uses and period remain undefined. USD 7.5m is an illustrative allocation, not approval. Keep supplier revenue, operator revenue and shareholder economics separate. The same infrastructure purchase may be an intercompany transaction and cannot be counted twice.

PROFIT AMBITION
Currency, beneficiary, target year and pre-tax / after-tax basis for annual profit of 20-30 million remain undefined. Reverse scenarios are not a year-one promise. Y2/Y3 represent conditional operating stages; demand, authorization, renewals, delivery, licensing and cost determine progression.

DECISIONS
Confirm primary / reserve markets and regional mandate; approve a 90-day work-package budget; identify funding entity and release gates; clarify the profit basis; define the evidence needed before pipeline enters a forecast.

EVIDENCE
Register material statements, definition checks, assumptions and actual acceptance separately. Arithmetic checks establish consistency, not buyer intent or commercial performance. The English and Chinese sites share the same numeric model. Brand scenes are AI-generated concepts, not project photographs.
